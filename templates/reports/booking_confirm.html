❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is online consultation as good as in-person?

A: Yes! Video consultation allows doctors to see you, ask questions, and provide diagnosis. Perfect for follow-ups and non-emergency care.

Q: How long is a consultation?

A: Usually 15-30 minutes. Doctor will examine, ask questions, and provide guidance + digital prescription.

Q: When will I get prescription?

A: Digital prescription is sent immediately after consultation. You can download and show to pharmacist.

Q: Can I cancel appointment?

A: Yes! Most platforms allow free cancellation up to 24 hours. Check terms on platform.

🔒 Your Data is Safe: All platforms use end-to-end encryption for video calls and store data securely. Your prescription and records are private and only accessible to you and your doctor.
